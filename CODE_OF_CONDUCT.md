# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project, and to feel safe doing so, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and say what you mean plainly.
- Respect other points of view and other ways of working.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and take feedback gracefully.
- Think about the people who will read your words. Many of us do not write in our first language.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information (such as an address or an email) without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In every project space (the repository, issues, discussions, chats, and events), and in public places when someone is acting for the project.

## Enforcement

To report a problem, write to **omkar.sahu2000@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the project's maintainer, kept private, and answered within a few days. The person who reports is not named to anyone else without their say.

The maintainer may warn, temporarily block, or permanently remove from the project's spaces anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, and may remove or edit comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow it. The reason is always given to the person concerned.

This project's reporting address will change to a project address once the project has its own domain.
